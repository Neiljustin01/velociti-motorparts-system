# Velociti's MotorParts – Parts, Fabrication & Service Management System

A web-based system that runs a motor parts store and welding fabrication shop in Inopacan, Leyte, Philippines, in one place: inventory, counter sales, service projects, payments, customer credit (utang), worker labor shares and reports. The top priority is inventory and profit monitoring.

**Live system:** https://velociti.avenra.tech (owner sign-in required)

![Velociti's MotorParts sign-in page](https://njperasportfolio.avenra.tech/images/systems/velociti.jpg)

## What it does

- **Inventory:** motor parts, surplus parts and ready-made fabrications, with stock ledger, rack locations, physical counts, low-stock alerts and FIFO costing
- **POS:** barcode or quick search, automatic totals and change, cash, GCash, bank transfer or credit, and printed receipts
- **Service projects:** mechanic, vulcanizing, welding and fabrication jobs with costs, amount charged and profit per project
- **Worker accounts:** labor shares, cash advances (vale) and daily payouts with payout slips
- **Customers:** purchase, project and motorcycle service history, with partial payments and credit balances
- **Reports and dashboard:** 21 reports on sales, profit, stock, workers and expenses, with print, PDF and CSV export
- **Security:** hashed passwords, login lockout, auto logout, password-approved voids, audit log and all-or-nothing saving

## Built with

PHP 8 · MySQL / MariaDB · Bootstrap 5 · Chart.js · DataTables · hosted on Hostinger
